Why Is Dryer Eating Socks? A Political Analysis

**WASHINGTON** — A top-secret government report leaked today said that the laundry dryer is a "hungry beast of political intrigue." For years, socks have been disappearing, but no one knew why. Now, the answer is in the dryer's tummy.

The report, called "Operation Sock-Guzzler," said that the dryers are eating the socks one by one. This is a very big problem for the nation. Many peoples are not having matching socks for work and this is a sign of chaos in the world. The report said, "We must have a treaty with the dryers or all socks will be gone."
